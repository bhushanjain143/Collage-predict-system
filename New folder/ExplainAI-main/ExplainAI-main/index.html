How this is different from other predictors

  • Transparent logic. We compare your percentile to published closing cutoffs (with category / seat-type rules) — not a black-box “guarantee”.

  • Counselling + scholarships. College suggestions and scholarship hints in one small app; always confirm on CET Cell, MahaDBT, and NSP.
