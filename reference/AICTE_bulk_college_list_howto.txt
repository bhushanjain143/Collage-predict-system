How to get the full ~8,800+ engineering (and other) colleges — Private / Government / Aided
===========================================================================================

The small sample in data/engineering_colleges_SAMPLE_JoSAA_tier.* is ONLY:
  23 IITs + 31 NITs + 25 statutory IIITs (names + cities + placeholders).

It does NOT contain thousands of private or state government colleges. Those must
come from the regulator's approved-institute database.

AICTE — approved institutes (bulk export)
-----------------------------------------
1) Open the AICTE "Approved Institutes" dashboard (search: "AICTE approved institutes dashboard"
   if the exact URL changes). The public portal is often:
   https://facilities.aicte-india.org/
   or the newer analytics / approval pages linked from https://www.aicte-india.org/

2) Use filters: Programme = Engineering / Technology (or your course), State, etc.

3) Use the site's **Export / Download to Excel** (or CSV) if offered. Policies and
   UI change — use whatever export the current portal provides.

4) Columns you get from AICTE typically include institute name, address, state,
   district, programme, intake — they will NOT automatically include JEE cutoffs,
   placement packages, or fees; you merge those from state counselling sites and
   each college's own PDFs.

Why your counts (6,611+ private, 2,265+ govt / aided) are not in our ZIP
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Those numbers are national aggregates from surveys / regulator summaries. Shipping
8,000+ rows with cutoffs and placements would require continuous scraping and would
still be wrong without per-year, per-exam, per-category context. The JoSAA sample is
a **starter template**; AICTE + state DTE sites are the **scale path**.

JoSAA "26 IIITs" vs our 25 IIIT rows
-----------------------------------
The Ministry's public PDF "list_iiits.pdf" (education.gov.in) enumerates **5 MOE +
20 PPP = 25** IIIT organisations. Some JoSAA brochures round or group institutes
differently and quote **26**; always take the **seat matrix / institute list PDF**
for your admission year on https://josaa.nic.in/ as the final authority.
