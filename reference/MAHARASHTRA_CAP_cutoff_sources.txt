Maharashtra — Engineering / Technology CAP: where round-wise cutoffs live
===========================================================================

Official publisher: State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra
  https://cetcell.mahacet.org/

Each admission cycle uses its own portal folder (examples — URLs change yearly):
  - DSE 2025-26 style: https://dse2025.mahacet.org.in/
  - FE 2024-25 archives often under: https://fe2024.mahacet.org/ (or current year sibling)

What to download per year
--------------------------
For the academic year you care about (e.g. 2025-26), look for PDFs named like:
  - CAP-I / CAP1 cutoff (engineering)
  - CAP-II, CAP-III, … (same pattern through CAP V if published)

Typical filename fragments (not guaranteed for every year):
  - ...CAP1_CutOff...pdf
  - ...CAP2_CutOff...pdf
  ... through CAP5

Inside each PDF, locate your institute (e.g. Vishwakarma Institute of Information
Technology — “VIIT”, Pune), programme (branch), seat category code (e.g. GOPEN,
LOPEN, …), and the **closing merit / percentile** published for that round.

Why this repo only ships a TEMPLATE
-----------------------------------
Cutoff PDFs are not stable machine-readable open data. Year, exact path, and
column layout change. The CSV/XLSX built by scripts/maha_cap_round_template.py
gives you **rows for 5 years × 5 CAP rounds** so you can **paste** official values
and paste the **PDF URL** you used into the source column.

VIIT naming
-----------
“VIIT” usually refers to **Vishwakarma Institute of Information Technology**,
Pune (affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University). Always match spelling and
**institute / choice code** to the seat matrix for that year — do not rely on
abbreviations alone.
